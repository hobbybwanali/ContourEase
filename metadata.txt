Features: select X/Y/Z columns, optional CRS transformation (Lat/Long to UTM or any CRS), create interpolated DEM (IDW or TIN), generate smooth contour lines labeled by elevation (Z), clip contours to a boundary polygon, export contours/boundary/points to DXF, index contours and professional styling. Ideal for geology, mining, topography and engineering. Excel support requires openpyxl or pandas.
